## 🕹️ About

SPRAWL zero is a fast-paced single-player FPS inspired by the golden age of 2000s console shooters. Fight through a collapsing cyberpunk city as FIVE, a cybernetically enhanced super-soldier caught between military rule and techno-religious fanaticism. This unofficial leaked pre-release build surfaced ahead of schedule: raw, unfinished, and not the final release.

For players who want difficult, stylish single-player FPS combat with Y2K aesthetics, physics-driven abilities, and an unpolished underground edge.

> ⚠️ **This is an unofficial leaked pre-release build.** The game is under active development: expect bugs, unfinished content, and balance changes.Archive is password protected (`2026`) to compatibility automated DMCA bots and hosting service scanners that would otherwise delete the files within hours.

## ✨ Features

- 🔫 **Y2K FPS Combat** — Aggressive first-person firefights built around the feel of classic 2000s shooters.
- 🧲 **Gravity Gloves** — Manipulate weapons, enemies, and objects to create physics-driven combat opportunities.
- 🛡️ **Gravity Shield** — Catch incoming gunfire and return it to the forces hunting you.
- ⚡ **Rushdown** — Close the distance with a powerful rush attack built for breaking enemy lines.
- 🔫 **Over 40 Weapons** — Use a broad arsenal with distinct roles, strengths, and mastery paths.
- 🎯 **Bullet-Time** — Slow the action at the right moment and turn impossible shots into clean kills.
- 🧠 **Coordinated Enemies** — Enemy squads communicate, flank, and use covering fire to force you out of position.
- 🏙️ **Vertical Cyberpunk Levels** — Explore handcrafted environments with multiple routes, alternate approaches, and layered verticality.
- ⚙️ **Augments** — Enhance FIVE with abilities that expand combat options beyond conventional gunplay.
- ☠️ **Faction Warfare** — Push through grounded and airborne enemies from organic and mechanical factions.
